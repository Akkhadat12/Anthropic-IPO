## Product and evidence contract

- **Scene IDs:** `cover`, `demand`, `retained`, `models`, `statements`, `capacity`, `scenarios`, `filing`, `close`. Preserve this order. The presenter controls every advance; there is no autoplay or time-based scene advance.
- **Navigation:** Spacebar advances one stop, `R` returns to cover, Escape closes an open information panel. On-screen primary targets perform the scene-specific action listed below. Provide a visible “Sources / caveats” control that opens an accessible overlay for current-scene citations. Keep keyboard focus, accessible names, and hit targets usable. If reduced motion is requested, keep the same destinations and readable final states with minimal movement. Repeated rapid input must settle on a valid scene without skipped/stacked transitions.
- **Narration and labels:** Spoken script can be Thai, but visible page text must be English-only. Target at most 12 visible words per scene beyond indispensable chart labels/data. A sources overlay can contain full citations, classification, and caveats. Avoid paragraphs, tiny caveats under charts, and decorative numerals mistaken for evidence.
- **Information hierarchy:** Put period, unit, and status beside each number. Visually distinguish “reported from filing seen by Reuters”, “preliminary Bloomberg”, “Anthropic stated”, “independent benchmark”, and “scenario”. Never use an invented IPO ticker/price, fake official seal, or a fabricated document screenshot.
- **Chart data:** Use [references/chart-data.csv](references/chart-data.csv) and 02's claim ledger. Values in the CSV are a data manifest, not a license to interpolate missing quarters or payment maturities. The site should load or derive chart values from a centralized data module with source IDs and caveats.

Keep core evidence in a safe central zone for 16:9 capture and mobile; do not allow images, labels, or interactive targets to clip at common laptop widths or phone portrait.
