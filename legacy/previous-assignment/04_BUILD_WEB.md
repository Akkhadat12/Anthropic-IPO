## Visual direction and motif

Aim for analytical clarity with a tangible sense of scale. The foreground should carry the argument: a recognizable Project Rainier facility, a paid work event, compute required per useful task, differing financial measurement bases, and a forward commitment whose cash timing remains uncertain. Keep contrast and negative space strong enough for a recorded 16:9 frame, with readable labels and stable focal hierarchy. Use visually distinct encodings for historical recognized results, company-stated annualized pace, reported preliminary adjusted results, and future obligations; provide textual basis labels so meaning never depends on color alone. Do not encode unknown training/inference shares as measured proportions.

The proposed motif is **two linked clocks**: customer-work economics can improve on a shorter cycle while frontier capacity is committed over longer horizons. Treat this as a conceptual relationship, not an actual measured financial segmentation. Use it where it helps the cover, Scene 5, and closing; do not force it into every scene.

The builder must compare **at least two materially different viable color directions** on a representative scene already containing its real foreground/labels in a real browser. Choose the direction that best supports the subject, contrast, and 16:9 recording legibility. Record the final stage, type, and accent hex values and a brief visual reason in the build note. The background must support narration and not become a decorative subject. This brief does not prescribe a stage color, named hue, or light/dark mode.
