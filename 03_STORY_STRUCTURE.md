# Anthropic IPO — story structure

**Status:** Owner-approved thesis, 29 September 2026. This is a spoken, presenter-led 3D web story plan for a later builder. It is not a script or a completed site. Read [02_RESEARCH_AND_ANALYSIS.md](02_RESEARCH_AND_ANALYSIS.md) for the claim ledger and [04_BUILD_WEB.md](04_BUILD_WEB.md) for scene and interaction specifications.

## Editorial contract

- **Audience:** A financially curious Thai-speaking owner presenting to a general business/investing audience. Spoken narration may be Thai. On-screen text must be brief and in English.
- **Central question:** Can the cash retained from Claude demand grow fast enough to carry serving, training, partner shares, and contracted compute capacity?
- **Approved thesis:** Revenue is accelerating, while minimum compute obligations extend far ahead. Model quality matters financially only when it sustains willingness to pay and improves total cost per completed customer task. This is a testable question, not an assertion that Anthropic will or will not become profitable.
- **Narrative form:** A path of demand enters a capacity ledger. Each beat opens another layer of the economics. The ending returns to the opening question with five public-filing proof gates.
- **Duration:** About 9 minutes 25 seconds including cover and close. Timing can vary with narration, but the complete story should remain within 8–12 minutes.
- **Evidence rule:** Use the source links below as of 29 September 2026. Reuters reports figures from a confidential filing that the team has not read; Bloomberg's Q2 number is preliminary. Replace these when a public S-1 becomes available. Do not turn annualized run-rate into booked revenue, operating loss into cash burn, or decade commitments into one-year spend.

## Narrative guardrails

1. Keep reported facts, company claims, benchmark results, our inferences, and scenarios visibly distinguishable. A small source/caveat control can open full notes without crowding the projected frame.
2. The 2025, Q1/Q2 2026, and May run-rate objects must show their own periods and units. A single unqualified growth line would be misleading.
3. Show the reported $518B as *at least* and *roughly a decade*; show the 80% as approximate. Do not imply an annual payment schedule or add an overlapping AWS figure.
4. Present Opus/Sonnet's strong capability and the contrary task-cost observation in the same beat. The latter does not reveal Anthropic's own gross margin.
5. Mark the private $965B post-money Series H valuation as background in the reading pack; the story must not recast it as an IPO offer price or independent fair value.
6. Update the whole chain—research, chart data, story, build brief, and PDFs—if a public S-1 or corrected source changes a major claim.
